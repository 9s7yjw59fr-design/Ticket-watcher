PrImA faCIe

Buy tickets

Suzie Miller

1/10

We rarely hear about rape cases because reporting them can be devastating for victims—and the prospect of a fair trial can feel almost impossible.

Criminal lawyer Tessa is ambitious, distinguished, and courageous. She has worked her way from a small town in England to the grand corridors of London’s courts, and now she shares her story with us.

Prima facie is a Latin expression meaning “at first sight” or “on the face of it.” But as a society, we could ask more, listen better, and look beyond what seems apparent.
